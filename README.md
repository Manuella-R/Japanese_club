### **Trivia Carousel System**
An interactive carousel showcasing 6 fascinating Japanese facts:
1.  **Crying Sumo Contest** - A unique tradition for babies' good fortune
2.  **Punctual Trains** - Average delay of only 54 seconds per year
3.  **Mount Fuji** - The summit is privately owned by a shrine
4.  **Slurping is Polite** - Shows appreciation for the food
5.  **55,000+ Convenience Stores** - Open 24/7 across Japan
6.  **Cherry Blossom Forecast** - Official predictions for sakura viewing

**Features:**
- Auto-advances every 8 seconds
- Navigate with arrow buttons or keyboard
- Dot indicators for quick access
- Smooth slide animations

### **Cycling Quotes System**
The footer displays Japanese wisdom that automatically cycles through 8 different quotes:
1. 一期一会 (Ichi-go ichi-e) - Treasure every encounter
2. 七転び八起き (Nana korobi ya oki) - Fall seven times, stand up eight
3. 花鳥風月 (Kachō fūgetsu) - Experience the beauty of nature
4. 温故知新 (Onko chishin) - Learn from the past
5. 十人十色 (Jū nin to iro) - To each their own
6. 猿も木から落ちる (Saru mo ki kara ochiru) - Everyone makes mistakes
7. 継続は力なり (Keizoku wa chikara nari) - Continuity is strength
8. 千里の道も一歩から (Senri no michi mo ippo kara) - A journey begins with a single step
